# Independent 4B heldout audit and three-model recommendation

## Verdict and recommended gate

**4B is the strongest accuracy candidate on this 12-case cohort, but not qualified for unattended routing or a production replacement.** Its three net strict-filename gains over Bonsai2 are real: remittance, meeting notice and the mixed-signature amendment. It also preserves the correct noisy-statement issuer that Qwen loses. However, correct filenames do not clear false recipient descriptions, hidden raw party-role errors, misleading date roles or the common missing-unit defect. The annual report still becomes ready under a generic recipient without its issuing company.

Keep the production backend unchanged. If evaluating a human-reviewed alternative, prioritize 4B over Qwen when accuracy merits substantially longer waits. Do not claim that a conditional fallback or automatic routing rule is validated by this experiment: no such routing policy was tested. Before unattended use or replacement, require fail-closed handling of unsupported recipients/subjects, controlling-date conditions, date-role contradictions and description units, followed by a separately frozen evaluation set. Do not tune these completed heldouts or revise their scores. A successful transport/security run is not this semantic acceptance gate.

## Provenance and aggregate verification

All 12 completed seed-17 cases were independently audited using source, recorded raw replies and final accepted outputs. Each scored reply was matched by native request hash to its recording exchange; four unscored warmups were excluded. All 164 exported files across n4ha/n4hb/n4hc/n4hd rehashed correctly. Saved collector validations pass with no new Defender 1116 detections. All 12 extracted source objects, native prompt-hash lists and effective request-body JSON values exactly match Bonsai2. Qwen parity was independently established in its audit. These controls support a paired comparison of the model/runtime combinations, not attribution to model weights alone.

## Metric blind spots and gain limits

4B's frozen accepted type 12/12, date value 12/12, date role 9/12 and party-role 11/11 are reproduced, not treated as full semantic truth. Party-role excludes the annual report's unmatched generic recipient and overlooks reversed distribution roles. Date-role misses annual execution, statement invoice and remittance notice, while gold masks separation/lease effective-label issues. Automated miscited-ID totals are 15 Bonsai2, 11 Qwen and 4 Bonsai4; fewer flagged citations do not validate the roles attached to the names. Description_factual/complete can pass false recipients, missing feet and omitted conditions.

4B preserves every strict Bonsai2 success and adds three; it preserves every strict Qwen success and adds noisy statement and mixed-signature amendment. This is a favorable paired filename result, not a blanket semantic dominance result: capital-call recipient text remains false, meeting still needs review, annual identity still unsafe-ready, and invoice subject is worse than Qwen's. No model eliminates the shared engine composition/acceptance errors.

Recommendation: retain Bonsai2 as the unchanged baseline/default pending separate acceptance work; retain 4B as the accuracy candidate for explicitly human-reviewed evaluation if approximately 89% more summed document-processing time is acceptable. Qwen offers no compelling tradeoff in this run: slower than Bonsai2 with two important unsafe-ready results, and lower filename accuracy than 4B. None is cleared here for unattended routing. Do not use this small heldout set to invent and self-validate a selective escalation rule.
