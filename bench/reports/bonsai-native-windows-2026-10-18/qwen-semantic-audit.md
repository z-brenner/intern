# Independent Qwen heldout audit and paired Bonsai2 comparison

All 12 completed seed-17 cases reviewed against extracted source, the actual scored raw response, and the accepted filename/description. Each scored response was matched by native request hash to its recording exchange; four unscored warmups were excluded. Extracted source objects, every native prompt-hash list, and effective transmitted request-body JSON values independently match Bonsai2 for all 12 cases. Export ledger hashes were also rechecked. This audit changes no prompt, gold, score or model output.

## Paired result

Qwen does **not establish a clear safety or overall quality improvement** over Bonsai2 on this small cohort. It gains one strict filename match net, but promotes two important errors to ready, retains common engine defects, and takes longer on all 12 matched documents. This is not evidence of universal model superiority or a production recommendation.

Independently recomputed Qwen counts, median, p95 and time sum from all reports. Median rises 16.57%, total document time 11.33%, and p95 2.91%; p95 with 12 observations is the maximum. These are document extraction/analysis times, excluding model startup, unscored warmup, guest/process/export overhead. The extraction sums are similar; the added aggregate time is in inference. Guest splits and startup conditions prevent treating these as cold-start or end-to-end throughput claims.

Strict paired changes: remittance and meeting notice become filename-correct; noisy statement becomes filename-incorrect. Annual report and mixed-signature amendment remain strict misses but change from Bonsai2 review to Qwen ready. The two Qwen unsafe-ready cases misidentify the annual report's subject and choose the amendment's earlier signature date. Bonsai2's single unsafe-ready case was the meeting notice's omitted issuer. These are materially different failure severities; counts alone hide that difference.

## Metric limits and provenance

Accepted Qwen type 12/12, date value 11/12, date role 8/10 eligible and party-role 10/12 are reproduced as frozen metrics, not semantic clearance. Date-role eligibility excludes annual report's accepted alternate date and the amendment's wrong date; this masks the annual report filing-role error. Party-role scoring largely tests accepted name/relation membership, not literal raw roles. Automated miscited-ID totals are 11 Qwen versus 15 Bonsai2; they do not establish that other citations support all asserted roles. Lexical description_factual fails to identify several errors above.

The preserved source contains all key dates, roles, units and conditions used in these findings. Major Qwen errors trace to raw interpretation/example contamination and downstream filtering/composition. Shared missing feet and separation/lease effective-label issues trace to engine acceptance/composition. No new tuning follows this audit. Full paired findings should accompany any model decision.
