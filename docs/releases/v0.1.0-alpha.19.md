# Intern v0.1.0-alpha.19

Intern is an alpha release for Windows 10 or 11, x86-64.

## Better readings

- **A date a spreadsheet works out with TODAY or NOW waits for your check.** A workbook whose date cell holds `=TODAY()` shows whatever day it was last saved, and a different day each time it is opened. Intern read that as the document's date and filed the name with no check: a blank invoice template among the test documents was filed this way. A name whose date is stated only in such cells now waits, and review says "The date comes from a spreadsheet formula that uses today's date, so it can change each time the file is opened." The name itself is not changed, and a date that is also typed anywhere else in the workbook is taken as before. This covers `.xlsx`, `.xlsm` and `.xls`, and formulas that use TODAY or NOW inside something else (`=TODAY()+30`). It does not cover `.ods` workbooks, or a cell that only refers to another cell holding such a formula. The cost: an invoice made from such a template and saved on the day it was issued has a right date and now waits too. Nothing changed on the 1,224 recorded test readings, which is expected: a recording keeps the text Intern read, not the workbook's formulas.

## In review

- **A click on a button that has just come under the pointer does nothing.** alpha.18's notes listed this as not fixed. The window changes by itself - Intern reads a document again, a document leaves the queue, the text you had typed is set aside above a new reading - and a button can arrive under the pointer while your hand is already on its way to something else. That click renamed every ready document, discarded what you had typed, or filed a reading nobody had looked at. A click or a tap on a button that acts - Approve & rename, Keep original, Rename all ready, Remove and its yes, Discard, Undo, Cancel processing, Retry, Analyze again, the party and date choices, and the other buttons that decide, file, remove or discard - is now taken only when the button has been under the pointer for half a second. Two cases are taken at once: an item of a menu you just opened, once the pointer has moved to it; and the next document's Approve & rename or Keep original when it is drawn where the last one's was, so approving one document after another with the pointer at rest works as before. In the browser build of the window, 331 clicks timed to land just after such a change, at window heights from 600 to 1080 pixels and two widths, sent no command; the same clicks on alpha.18, at the two heights where it was run, sent 30 commands of 66. The same click made after the half second worked 163 times of 163. Keys are not changed by this.
- **What that costs.** Nothing on screen says a click was not taken: look at what is under the pointer now, then click again. After the half second, a click there acts on whatever is there, whether or not it is what you first meant. A click made within about a third of a second of one that was not taken does nothing either - otherwise the second click of a double-click would act on what the first was kept from - so clicking faster than about three times a second gets nothing, for as long as you go on, until you stop for a moment. alpha.18 took the first such click after the half second. And if you press any key, Shift alone included, between a click that was not taken and the next, Approve & rename, Keep original, Remove and the Discard beside what you had typed do nothing on that next click when it follows within the double-click time: a key can have decided the document, and the click would land on the one after it. In a tall window, when a command is answered late, the next document's buttons can be drawn in another place than the last one's, and a click less than half a second after they show is not taken: at one click a second, in a window 1400 by 900, with answers that took 600 ms, 2 clicks of 6 were not. All of this was measured in the browser build; nobody has yet run the Windows app's own window with it.
- **What you typed in review and did not file is kept when Intern closes.** alpha.18 kept it only while Intern stayed open: closing Intern, a crash, or the restart an update makes threw it away with no notice. It is now stored as you type, on this computer only, in the window's own storage, and removed when you file the document, keep its original name, remove it, have it read again, discard what you typed or go back to Intern's proposal, and when the document is no longer in the queue. Rename all ready leaves a document with such text alone, as it did, and the cursor is put in no field because of it.
- **On Windows, expect it beside the document, read-only, not back in its fields.** It goes back into the fields only when Intern can tell that its window closed in the ordinary way and the document still has the reading you typed over. Otherwise it is shown beside the document as "What you had typed", to copy from or discard, so that a name from an earlier moment is never in a field where Enter would file it. Quit Intern in the notification area and the restart an update makes end Intern without closing its window, and where Intern keeps running in the background, closing the window only hides it; whether the Windows app records an ordinary close at all has not been observed. After Intern was killed, or the computer lost power or was shut down with Intern open, what you typed in the last half minute or more can be missing, and a name you had taken back or filed can be there again, beside the document. One case can still put an older name back in its field: Intern closed in the ordinary way, opened again, a name taken back in the first seconds after opening, and Intern killed at once. Look at a name that came back before you file it.

## On documents it has never seen

Before this release, 55 more public documents were gathered and their correct names written down by hand, read again blind by a second reader, and what would be measured on them was fixed in advance. Nobody who wrote or reviewed this release's rules opened them, and this time the briefs they worked from were written before the set existed. The accurate model read all 55, once, after the release's one engine rule was final:

- **16 of 54 names right**, the same as alpha.18's engine gives from the same replies: the two read every one of the 55 the same way. Type right on 43 of 55, date on 41 of 54, parties on 40 of 55.
- **4 names filed without your check, 3 right and 1 wrong.** The wrong one is a company's application to be struck off the register, filed as an "Off Application": Intern cut the first word from the form's title. alpha.18 files it the same way. Nothing was changed for it, because a fix written from these documents would be fitted to them; it is the next engine work, and will be reported as such.
- 93% of the 55 waited for a check, and 13 of the 16 right names were among them. Expect most documents to wait for a quick look.
- 16 of 54 is fewer than the 19 of 51 the previous set gave under the same engine. They are different documents; neither set can certify a rate.

## Updates

The installer is `Intern_0.1.0-alpha.19_x64-setup.exe`. Existing settings, queue records and model downloads are retained; upgrading from alpha.13 or later downloads no model.

The installer is **not Authenticode signed**. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. Updater signatures, checksums and GitHub build provenance are verified before publication.
